About us

Beauty, care, and a little time for you.

Escape Nails Spa is a welcoming nail salon in Springfield, MO. Whether you want a classic manicure, a relaxing spa pedicure, or a full set of Gel-X, acrylic or builder gel nails, our team takes the time to get the shape, length and color just right.

Frequently Asked Questions

Where is Escape Nails Spa located?

Escape Nails Spa is at 1312 E Battlefield Rd, Springfield, MO 65804. We welcome clients from Springfield, Nixa, Ozark, Republic, Battlefield, Strafford and Willard.

What nail services do you offer in Springfield, MO?

We offer manicures, classic to signature spa pedicures, Gel-X nail extensions, acrylic nails, builder gel and custom nail art.

What are your hours?

We are open Monday, Wednesday, Thursday and Friday from 9:00 AM to 7:00 PM, Saturday from 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Sunday from 11:00 AM to 4:00 PM, and closed Tuesday.

Do you accept walk-ins?

Call (417) 693-5354 to book an appointment or to ask about walk-in availability.

How much do Gel-X and acrylic nails cost?

A Gel-X full set is $65, an acrylic full set is $55 and a builder gel full set is $50. Extra length and nail art start at $5+, so ask your technician before starting.
